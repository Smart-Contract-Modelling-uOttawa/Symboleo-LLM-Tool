EQUIPMENT LOAN AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Northwind Tooling Ltd. ("the Lender") and Cedar
Fabrication Inc. ("the Borrower"), and takes effect on the Start Date.

1. Loan of Equipment. The Lender shall hand over to the Borrower one calibration
   kit, bearing the serial number recorded in this Agreement. The kit remains the
   property of the Lender for the duration of the loan.

2. Return. The Borrower shall return the kit to the Lender on or before the
   Return Date, being the Start Date plus the number of loan days specified in
   this Agreement.

3. Late Fee. If the Borrower fails to return the kit by the Return Date, the
   Borrower shall pay the Lender the late fee specified in this Agreement.

4. Termination. If the Borrower fails to pay the late fee, the Lender may
   terminate this Agreement.

5. Depot Access. The Lender grants the operator of the storage depot permission
   to read the serial number of the kit and the status of the Borrower's return
   obligation, for the purpose of verifying custody.
